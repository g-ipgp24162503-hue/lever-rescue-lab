🧠 Page 3 — The Big Question

👩‍🔬
Dr. Maya: Now you know the three parts of a lever. Look carefully at the two setups below. The same rock (LOAD) stays on the right end of the beam, and the FORCE is applied on the left end. Only the ▲ FULCRUM changes position.
Scientific question: How does the distance between the LOAD and the FULCRUM affect the force needed to lift the load?
Which setup do you predict will need less force? This is a prediction, so it will not be marked wrong.
